A story shaped over generations

Rooted in heritage.
Made for what’s next.

With deep Italian roots, Union Tiles was established in Johannesburg in 1922 as a terrazzo tile and concrete products manufacturer. After moving to Wychwood in 1950, the company made Bedfordview its home in 1968.

Today, the Bedfordview head office remains a central part of the business, with a showroom and a wide selection of wall and floor finishes for homes and commercial projects.
